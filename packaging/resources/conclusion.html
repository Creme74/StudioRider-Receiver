StudioRider Receiver is installed

  1. In Logic Pro, create a software instrument track and load StudioRider Receiver as its instrument (AU MIDI-controlled Effects).
  2. Paste the link the studio sent you, click Connect.
  3. To record what you hear, send that track to a bus and record the bus on an audio track.

If Logic Pro was open, quit and reopen it.

To uninstall: Applications > StudioRider Receiver > Uninstall StudioRider Receiver.
