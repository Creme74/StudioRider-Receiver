StudioRider Receiver @VERSION@

A companion to StudioRider: it plays a StudioRider listening link inside your DAW, so you can hear it next to your own tracks or record it. It does nothing on its own: you need the link a studio sends you from StudioRider. To just listen, a web browser is enough and nothing needs installing.

Testing phase. To date, StudioRider Receiver has been tested and works only in Logic Pro, as an Audio Unit. Ableton Live doesn't work yet.

This installs, for all users of this Mac:

  • the Audio Unit, for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs;
  • an uninstaller, in Applications > StudioRider Receiver.

Copies of StudioRider Receiver in your own ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins folder are replaced by this one. StudioRider itself is not touched.
